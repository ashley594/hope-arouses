Warmest welcome!

Hello dearest friends and family,

First, a warm welcome and a sincerest thank you for the support you've shown us over the past few months. We feel it, and we're excited to invite you to participate in this next phase of building Hope Arouses.

Before this goes out to hundreds of women, we want to make sure we're asking the right questions. Your answers will help us refine them so we can get the information we need to make important decisions about our business. All we ask is that you answer as honestly as you can — and if the honest answer is “I've never thought about this,” that's genuinely useful to us too. If a question is confusing, or none of the options quite fit you, please text us or leave a comment. That's exactly the kind of feedback we're hoping for.

It takes 15 minutes tops, and a few of the questions get personal — sizing, measurements, money. Though we hope you answer each question, every single one is optional, so please skip anything you'd rather not answer.

We are in the middle of finalizing our underwear and bralette patterns and are working to find the right partners to bring our bras and underwear to the world. The information from this survey will help us build a size range that reflects real bodies instead of guessing, get the fit right, select the right fabrics, place the right sizing orders with our partners, price things appropriately, and find the people who are looking for what we're building.

Thank you, truly. We're building our dream company and are so glad to bring you along into our process.

With hope and love,
Ashley + Olivia, your friends and Co-Founders of Hope Arouses
